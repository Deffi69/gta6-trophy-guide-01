# Research notes & sources — GTA VI trophy list

Researched 2026-11-20 (game released 19 Nov 2026). Rockstar Games has not
published an official trophy list of its own; the list below is the PlayStation
PS5 list as it appears on the platforms, collected by PSNProfiles and
TrueAchievements.

## The list, verified

- **55 trophies on PS5: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze.** Xbox has the
  same 55 as achievements, worth 1,000 Gamerscore; the Platinum is a 0G Mythic
  achievement there.
- **27 visible, 28 hidden** (12 of the hidden are story trophies: 1 Gold + 11
  Bronze; the other 16 hidden name side characters and places).
- **All single-player, all offline — 0 online trophies**, no difficulty
  trophies, no mission medals.
- **Platinum:** "Vice Got a Price!" — "Congratulations! You're a Leonida Legend."
- **Golds:** "A Long Road" (100% completion) and "You Gotta Know When to Get Off"
  (final mission).
- **Only possibly missable trophy:** "Positive Mental Attitude" — Lucia's
  balcony meditation in Chapter 1 disappears after that chapter (PowerPyx via
  Output Lag / KeenGamer / GTA Zone, all agreeing).
- **No mission replay, no New Game Plus.** 15 manual save slots + 1 autosave.
  Keep a save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.
- **Cheats switch off trophies and saving** on a trophy save.
- **Platinum time:** ~200 hours (PowerPyx); Push Square's guide says up to
  300+ hours. Both are early estimates, not measured averages.

Every name, description and grade in `app/data/trophies.json` was checked
against the starter kit's list (dated 2026-11-18) and against the sources
below — all 55 match exactly, with zero mismatches.

## Counts used in the guides (all from Output Lag unless noted)

- Stunts: 250 for the trophy, 300 for 100%. Challenges: 50 of 80 for the
  trophy, 70 for 100%. Vehicles: 100 discovered for the trophy, 200 of 277
  base models for 100%. Shooting Range: 2 ranges, 42 gold medals. Top Fun
  drone races: 14 locations. Bank vaults: 5 for the trophy, 8 for 100%.
  Illegal operations: 1 per gang (9 gangs) for the trophy, all 39 for 100%.
  Garages: 8, ~$160,000 total. Marked Bills: $250,000 cumulative.
- 100% checklist appears only after the story (Progress > Stats > 100%
  Completion), but earlier progress counts.
- Character switching opens after Breakfast Run (Chapter 2).
- Bank vaults unlock via "That's Where The Money Is" (optional, near the end
  of Chapter 4). Jewelry robberies need the Snap Gun from Yasmani's Jewelry
  Jobs (Chapter 3). Scooter Bros app opens with the mission Moses (Chapter 2);
  garages via the Vice City Real Estate app after Daily Driver (Chapter 2).
- Combat Focus: L3+R3 while aiming; each fish landed adds ~10% to the meter.
- VCIA planes that count: docked at a boarding bridge, gates E3 and E4.
  Tanks: south side of Mile Zero Air Station, Goose Key.
- Strangers & Freaks: Andy's, Billy's and Tyrone's strands do NOT count (they
  have their own trophies); check the trophy's progress counter after each
  strand.

## Honesty notes

- "The Truth Is Out There" and "Bad Luck, I Guess" (and the exact spots for
  "In The Beginning…" and "Capital Offense") have **no confirmed unlock method
  in any guide yet** — the app says so plainly instead of inventing steps.
  KeenGamer lists The Truth Is Out There as "hidden trophy with an
  unconfirmed unlock method".
