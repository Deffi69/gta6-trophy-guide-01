⚠ One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude needs Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, which disappears when the chapter ends. There is no mission replay — keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Everything else can be finished after the story.

Road to the Platinum

Roughly 200 hours all in, say the early guides. No online trophies, no difficulty trophies, no mission medals — the cost is time, not skill. Work the stages in order; anything outside the story stays open after the credits.

    The 100% checklist appears only after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — but progress you made earlier still counts. Never use cheats on your trophy save: they switch trophies and saving off.
